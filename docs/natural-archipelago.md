# Natural archipelago and rocky shores

The supplied layout reference guides a less regular island formation: an indented central coast, a larger north-west headland, a northern island, a south-east blossom island, a crescent in the south-west, an autumn island to the south and two smaller eastern islets. Four tiny offshore keys add asymmetry and scale. Three curved sandy necks connect the north-west, north and south-east islands; other islands remain across water. Main-island eastern terraces are greener while regional sand, woodland and mountain palettes remain.

All coastlines, keys and beach connections use the existing sculptable 20-layer terrain. Navigable routes across the connections respect body clearance and single-layer climbing. Hills retain stepped relief and broad settlement terraces. Coastal boulders are faceted, clustered, grey-green rock meshes anchored in shallow water, with separate animated foam collars. Their whole footprint stays off dry terrain and out of designated fishing water. Raising the seabed removes the rocks and foam. Nine instanced scenery batches include foliage, rocks and foam; no extra individual rock draw calls or gameplay obstacles are introduced.

Save format 10 automatically reshapes untouched terrain from format 9 using its frozen generator as the baseline. Earlier formats use their existing mainland baseline. Existing villages, buildings, orders, resources, trails, fauna, fishing water and current follower routes have protected buffers. Sculpted samples and immediate neighbours are kept. Untouched former islands may be lowered while the new layout is added; occupied or edited areas retain the player's geography, so an existing developed world can differ from the complete default layout. On startup the previous raw save is backed up under the before-natural-coast key. Saved format-10 terrain remains authoritative on subsequent loads. No population, job, cargo, food or livestock data is regenerated.

Validation: automated terrain, settlement, food and navigation suites; natural island topology and safe beach connections; format-9 migration with preserved sculpting and world state; idempotent loading; coastal rock placement and removal after raising land. TypeScript, focused game lint, local route and production build are checked. Browser visual and device performance testing were not requested.
