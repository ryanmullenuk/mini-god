# Natural coast, regional trees and sound

The broad mainland now has asymmetric headlands and three substantial coastal inlets. Three offshore keys remain, with irregular outlines. The existing terrain grid, brush-local rebuilding and broad central construction terraces are unchanged. The regression suite still requires four disconnected land masses, over 15,000 safe flat construction samples, and fewer than one million terrain vertices.

Vegetation selection is separate from historical terrain generation so older save comparisons remain stable. Existing instanced tree models and density budgets are reused.

One sea source and a low-frequency scheduler avoid extra rendering work. The mute preference persists on this device. Pause and hidden tabs suspend audio; teardown clears the scheduler and closes its context. These are synthesized ambience rather than field recordings.
